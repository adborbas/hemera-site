# Privacy Policy

**Hemera**
Last updated: April 7, 2026

## Overview

Hemera is a companion app for Home Assistant. It connects directly to a Home Assistant server that you provide and control. We do not operate any servers or services on your behalf.

Hemera is [open source](https://github.com/adborbas/Hemera) under the MIT License — you can review the source code to verify these privacy claims yourself.

## Data Collection

Hemera does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party.

## Network Communication

All network communication occurs exclusively between your device and the Home Assistant server you configure. This includes:

- WebSocket connections for real-time device state updates
- REST API calls for retrieving area and entity information
- Service calls for controlling your smart home devices

No data from these communications is sent anywhere other than your own Home Assistant instance.

## Local Storage

Hemera stores the following data locally on your device:

- Your Home Assistant server URL and authentication token (stored securely in the device Keychain)
- Entity and area data synced from your Home Assistant instance
- Your dashboard layout preferences

This data never leaves your device except to communicate with your Home Assistant server.

## Third-Party Services

Hemera does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Tracking

Hemera does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Children's Privacy

Hemera does not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date.
