# Support

For questions, feedback, or support requests, email us at [hemera.app@proton.me](mailto:hemera.app@proton.me).

## Bug Reports & Feature Requests

Hemera is open source. The best way to report a bug or request a feature is through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/adborbas/Hemera/issues).

## FAQ

**How do I connect Hemera to my Home Assistant?**
Open the app and enter your Home Assistant server URL (e.g., `https://homeassistant.local:8123`). You'll be guided through authentication via your Home Assistant instance.

**Does Hemera work without an internet connection?**
Hemera communicates directly with your Home Assistant server over your local network. If your server is reachable on your local network, no internet connection is needed.

**Is my data sent to any third party?**
No. Hemera communicates exclusively with your Home Assistant server. There are no analytics, tracking, or third-party services. See our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details.

**Is Hemera free?**
Yes. Hemera is free and open source under the MIT License.

**Where can I find the source code?**
On GitHub: [github.com/adborbas/Hemera](https://github.com/adborbas/Hemera)
